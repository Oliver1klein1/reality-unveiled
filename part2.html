Part 2

Evidence for Non-Local Consciousness

Here's where things get really interesting. Now that we've blown up the materialist model, let's look at what the evidence is actually telling us about where consciousness lives. People who've been clinically dead are coming back with stories that would make physicists check their math. Kids are remembering lives they've never lived. Memories are showing up in places they shouldn't exist ... like in someone else's transplanted heart. The data keeps pointing to one uncomfortable truth: your consciousness isn't locked in your skull. It extends beyond space, beyond time, and apparently beyond death itself.
